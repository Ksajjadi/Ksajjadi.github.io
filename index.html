Research

I'm currently working on three research projects at the University of Tehran, spanning AI for software engineering, multimodal computer vision, and AI in education. Manuscripts for the first two are targeted for September/October 2026.

TaD-REV: Task- and Domain-Grounded, Evidence-Validated Stateful Review of Code Changes

B.Sc. Thesis · Supervised by Prof. Ramtin Khosravi · Oct 2025 – Present

A commit-level LLM code review pipeline grounded in task specifications, rubrics, domain documents, and repository baselines. It preserves first-pass and evidence-validated outputs as structured artifacts for every reviewed commit. The study runs an F0/I0/I1 design over authentic University of Tehran commit histories, with stable finding IDs, a validated cross-commit ledger, full telemetry, and a 75-finding double-annotated sample.

HARBOR: Post-Earthquake Building Damage Assessment from Pre-Event Optical and Post-Event SAR Imagery

With Dr. Ahmad Toosi, under Prof. Farhad Samadzadegan · Oct 2025 – Present

Benchmarks U-Net and ChangeMamba on 373 earthquake test tiles. Building localization reaches 91.56% F1, but damage classification drops to 32.77%, with 74.4% of damaged and 54.8% of destroyed buildings predicted as intact. I'm driving a three-stage architecture built around an affected-building gate, reliability-gated optical–SAR alignment, and object-region pooling to close that gap.

LLM-Simulated Persian Students for Evaluating AI Tutoring and Personalization

Supervised by Prof. Majid Nili Ahmadabadi · Feb 2026 – Present

LLM-based Persian student personas with bounded knowledge, stable misconceptions, confidence, engagement, and help-seeking behavior, built to evaluate an AI tutor before enough real dialogue exists. I'm building a black-box harness for 5–10-turn LMS sessions that records transcripts and metadata for persona consistency, misconception stability, and tutor-strategy analysis. The output will be a controlled dialogue dataset for evaluation and future personalization work.
